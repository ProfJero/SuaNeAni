Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! Sua Ne Ani is registered in multiple countries. We provide receipts for tax purposes in the US, UK, Ghana, and other jurisdictions.

How is my donation used?

Your donation directly supports our education programs, teacher training, community projects, and operational costs. We maintain 100% transparency.

Can I donate in my local currency?

Yes! We accept donations in multiple currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, GHS, NGN, and more. Our system automatically converts.

Can I sponsor a child's education?

Absolutely! We have a child sponsorship program. Contact us at sponsor@suaneani.org for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, mobile money, and cryptocurrency.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes! You will receive an email receipt immediately after your donation. For tax purposes, we can provide formal receipts upon request.
