1. Introduction

Welcome to SuaNeAni ("we," "our," or "us"), operated via SuaNeAni Education and Development Foundation, Accra, Ghana. We are dedicated to empowering minds, bridging the educational gap, and transforming lives through digital learning solutions in Ghana.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our platform, or engage with our programs, forms, or partner initiatives.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or describes you ("Personal Data"). This includes:

Directly Provided Data

  • Contact information (e.g., name, email address, phone number, organization name).
  • Inquiries submitted via contact forms, questionnaires, or interest forms (for donors, hardware manufacturers, content providers, NGOs, or volunteers).
  • Information provided when registering as an educator, student, or partner.

Automatically Collected Data

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers.
  • Usage data, such as pages visited, time spent on the platform, and referral links.

Child Data & Protection

Since our digital learning solutions benefit children, we prioritize child online privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 18 without explicit consent from a parent, guardian, or authorized educational institution.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate digital learning services, hardware access, and educational initiatives.
  • To communicate with partners, donors, educators, and volunteers regarding initiatives, deployment, and updates.
  • To process inquiries, partnerships, and impact contributions.
  • To analyze website traffic, platform usage, and optimize user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and safeguard our rights and platform security.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers & Partners: With trusted technology partners, hardware providers, or educational NGOs collaborating with SuaNeAni to deliver our programs (under strict confidentiality agreements).
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or governmental regulations, including Ghana's Data Protection Commission standards.
  • Organizational Changes: In connection with any merger, consolidation, or restructuring of our organization or initiatives.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to secure your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable law.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location and subject to Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and international regulations, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain data processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@suaneani.com.
